Balancing chemical equations

Balance simple chemical equations by adjusting coefficients, applying conservation of mass.

L. Chen
What worked
  • Checking for understanding was exemplary — mini-whiteboards plus two cold calls sampled well beyond the confident volunteers.
  • Explanation was clearly modelled, with thinking made visible step by step.
  • Specific, reasoning-based praise (“I like how you used an estimate to check”) reinforced the right moves.
Try next lesson
  1. Open with a 3-minute retrieval quiz on last lesson's content before moving to new material.
  2. Swap generic praise for specific feedback naming exactly what the student did well.
  3. Keep cold-calling after hands-up questions, not just for check-ins — spread it through the explanation too.

Transparency

The rubric behind every score

RELAY scores seven dimensions drawn from well-replicated research on effective instruction. Each is scored 1–4 with quoted evidence from the transcript. Weights reflect how reliably each can be judged from audio, not how much it matters.

Supported formats
Audio recording
From an external recorder or lapel mic. Transcribed with speaker diarization — teacher and student talk are separated automatically.
WebVTT / SRT
Exported by most recorders and transcription services. Timings are used for wait-time analysis.
Timestamped lines
[00:03:20] T: text
Plain text
Works, but timings are estimated so wait time can't be measured.
